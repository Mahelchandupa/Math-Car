🏁 Game Guide & Coin Rewards 🪙

🎯 How To Play

Steer your car using A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys. Avoid road obstacles and defeat your rival racer to cross the finish line first!

Answer math questions quickly using your mouse or keys [1], [2], [3] to boost your speed. Wrong answers decelerate your car.

🔥 Streak Bonus: Answer 3 in a row correctly for an extra +10 Coins!

🏎️ Unlocking Cars & Local Storage

Car 1 is free for all racers! Use your earned coins to purchase high-tier racing cars from the garage. Your unlocked cars, coins balance, and driving statistics are permanently saved in your browser's Local Storage.
