🏁 Game Guide & Coin Rewards 🪙

🎯 How To Play

Steer your car using A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys. Line up at the Starting Line alongside your rival opponents and race to cross the finish line first!

Answer math questions quickly using your mouse or keys [1], [2], [3] to boost your speed. Make sure not to let any opponent car reach the finish line before you!
